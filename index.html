ABOUT

/

Amax is a Thailand-based motion designer specializing in fluid animation and precise audio-visual synchronization, operating as a versatile solo creator who independently builds and composites both custom 3D and 2D assets.

While his design process begins by studying and merging diverse references, he is constantly experimenting to break away from conventional formulas and craft a unique visual style.

Although complex narrative storytelling is an area he is still actively developing, his core strength lies in delivering powerful aesthetic impact and rhythmic flow.

To maintain high-quality output and manage burnout, Amax adopts a sustainable, quality-over-speed approach that strictly prioritizes his physical and mental well-being; consequently, his project timelines are carefully paced—ranging from a single day to over a year—ensuring every piece receives the dedicated time, rest, and meticulous care it truly requires.

Amax is a Thailand-based motion designer.

Starting his creative journey in AMV editing a few years ago, he developed a deep fascination with motion design. He draws continuous inspiration from creative videos across Japan and China. While he started as an admirer from afar, his editing style is heavily influenced by these video, and he hopes to connect with those creators in the future. In late 2025, he expanded his workflow into 3D production for the first time, exploring new dimensions of visual depth and camera dynamics.

His creative interests also extend into the virtual club scene. After experiencing Japanese clubs within VRChat, he developed a passion for DJing and VJing. His dream is to gain hands-on experience within VRChat clubs and eventually build a virtual club of his own.

Beyond motion design, he has a wide range of interests, including music production, 3D modeling, and illustration. His original goal was to become a game developer; while that specific ambition has faded over time, he lives by the principle that once a goal is set, it should never be abandoned and he views his current interests as things that can all be leveraged to help him achieve his goal of becoming a game developer.

Over the past four years, his portfolio has grown through select commissions, personal projects, and creative collaborations. While still humble in scale, his work on social media reflects his genuine dedication to the craft—always creating in hopes of connecting with those who appreciate his vision.
